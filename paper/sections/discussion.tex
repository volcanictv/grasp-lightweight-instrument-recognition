\section{Discussion and limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

\paragraph{Ground-truth boxes.}
All results assume the box of every instrument is given.
Strong detectors exist (TAPIS reports 89.85 box mAP@0.5 on this data), so we studied recognition given boxes and left detector integration to future work.
Detector errors will lower our scores by an amount we did not measure, and the comparison with TAPIS, which finds its own boxes, is an upper bound for a detector-fed pipeline; the IoU margin is the most inflated by this because spurious classes count against a method that has to detect.

\paragraph{Scope of the evidence.}
Everything is measured on one dataset with five test cases, so the intervals on mcIoU in particular are wide.
Each segmenter was trained once; the spread reported covers the classifier only.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
One pass of an evidential ensemble is enough to decide which instruments need a temporal correction, and with fine-tuned SAM2 and SAM3 masks the result reaches 87.4 mIoU, 86.2 IoU and 78.3 mcIoU on GraSP with given boxes.
A causal variant keeps most of the accuracy with a small segmenter and a causal tracker.
