\paragraph{Instrument segmentation and recognition.}

\paragraph{Promptable segmentation.}
SAM2 \citep{ravi2024sam2} segments and propagates masks through video from a box or mask prompt, and SAM3 \citep{carion2025sam3} extends the family.
We use both as box-prompted segmenters and use SAM2 for propagation; EdgeTAM \citep{zhou2025edgetam} is a lighter tracker that we use for the causal variant.

\paragraph{Uncertainty.}
MC dropout \citep{gal2016dropout} estimates uncertainty from stochastic forward passes, and softmax confidence is the usual baseline; both are known to be poorly calibrated under shift \citep{guo2017calibration,ovadia2019trust}.
Evidential deep learning \citep{sensoy2018evidential} places a Dirichlet distribution over the class probabilities in one pass, and \citet{duan2024evidential} give variance-based scores that separate epistemic from aleatoric uncertainty; we use their epistemic score.
