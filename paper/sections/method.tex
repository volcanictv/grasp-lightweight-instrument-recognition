\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

Given a frame $I$ and the boxes $\{b_k\}$ of its instruments, the pipeline returns for each instrument a mask $\hat{m}_k$, a class $\hat{y}_k \in \{1,\dots,C\}$ with $C=7$, and an uncertainty score $S_k$ (Figure~\ref{fig:pipeline}).

\subsection{Box-prompted segmenter}
SAM2.1-large and SAM3 are each fine-tuned on GraSP with the ground-truth box as prompt and a mask loss.
For SAM2 we train the mask decoder, the prompt encoder, the neck and the last four encoder blocks (267\,MB of changed weights); for SAM3 only the mask decoder and prompt encoder change (15\,MB).
At inference each model predicts mask logits for the image and its mirror image, the four logit maps are averaged, and the mask is the region where the average is positive.
Checkpoints and the use of mirror averaging were chosen on a held-out fold; the final models are then trained on all training cases with the fixed schedule that selection produced.

\subsection{Evidential ensemble classifier}
The mask multiplies the frame inside the box, and the crop is passed to four networks: ResNet-50 at 320\,px and at 224\,px, and two MobileNetV3-small networks (one with a stretched crop, one with a letterboxed crop).
Each network output $z_j \in \mathbb{R}^C$ is read as Dirichlet evidence,
\begin{equation}
\alpha_j = \exp\!\big(\mathrm{clip}(z_j,-10,10)\big) + 1,
\label{eq:alpha}
\end{equation}
without a softmax.
The ensemble parameter is the weighted mean $\alpha = \sum_j w_j \alpha_j$ with $w = (0.40, 0.20, 0.20, 0.20)$; with $\alpha_0=\sum_c \alpha_c$ and $\mu = \alpha/\alpha_0$, the prediction is $\hat{y}=\arg\max_c \mu_c$ and the uncertainty is the epistemic score of \citet{duan2024evidential},
\begin{equation}
S = \frac{1 - \sum_c \mu_c^2}{\alpha_0 + 1}.
\label{eq:score}
\end{equation}
Members are trained with the evidential loss $\sum_c y_c(\log\alpha_0 - \log\alpha_c) + \lambda\,\mathrm{KL}\big(\mathrm{Dir}(\tilde{\alpha})\,\|\,\mathrm{Dir}(\mathbf{1})\big)$ with $\tilde{\alpha}=y+(1-y)\alpha$, $\lambda=0.01$ annealed over ten epochs (chosen on the held-out fold by macro-F1 alone).

\paragraph{Tracker-style crops.}
Crops produced by a tracker differ from crops made with a clean mask.
During training, with probability 0.5 the crop of an instrument is replaced by a stored EdgeTAM crop of the same instrument one to five frames away.

\subsection{Evidence gate and temporal correction}
An instrument is sent to correction when $S\ge\tau$, or, to fix the cost, when it is among the $K$ highest scores.
Its mask is propagated by SAM2-large over the ten frames on each side of the keyframe, the ensemble classifies the instrument on every propagated frame $f$ to give $\mu_f$, and the frames are fused with confidence weights,
\begin{equation}
\hat{y} = \arg\max_c \sum_f \big(\max_{c'} \mu_{f,c'}\big)\, \mu_{f,c}.
\label{eq:fuse}
\end{equation}
Instruments below the gate keep their single-frame class.
The threshold $\tau$ is chosen on a held-out fold: sweep it with tracking, take the highest accuracy, then the cheapest gate within 0.001 of it.

\subsection{Causal real-time variant}
\label{sec:causalmethod}
The correction above uses frames on both sides of the keyframe.
The causal variant uses only the past: SAM2-tiny with mirror averaging replaces the segmenter ensemble, and the propagation runs backward from the keyframe over the 20 preceding frames with a causal tracker (EdgeTAM; SAM2-large and a YOLO26s-seg tracker are compared in Section~\ref{sec:realtime}).
Frames arrive at one per second, the single-frame label is available immediately, and the corrected label follows when the tracker finishes.
