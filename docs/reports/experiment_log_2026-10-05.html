GraSP Experiment Log

Every experiment attempted, oldest first, with the numbers and what happened to it. For reference only, kept apart from the reports. Numbers come from the decision log, the result files and the runs themselves; accuracy and macro-F1 are on the official test set unless a row says fold1 or fold2.

Where the pipeline is today

  • Classifier: four-member evidential ensemble (ResNet-50 at 320, ResNet-50 at 224, two MobileNetV3), weights 0.40 / 0.20 / 0.20 / 0.20, three seeds. The tracker-style-crop (arm N) versions are queued to replace the baseline members.
  • Gate and tracker: top 833 instruments by epistemic score, SAM2-large tracking of those, evidence-weighted vote over the track.
  • Segmentor for the PI comparison: ground-truth boxes into SAM2 plus SAM3, fine-tuned on GraSP, equal-weight logit ensemble. Scored with mIoU, IoU and mcIoU against TAPIS. Oracle-box setting, always stated.

Verdicts: Used is in the current pipeline or its ensemble; Used, then replaced was shipped for a while; No gain and Harmful were tried and dropped (harmful means clearly worse than the baseline it was compared with); Finding is a measurement or decision that shaped later work; Queued or running has no result yet. Early detection and segmentation numbers (August 31 to September 2) were validated on the official test set before held-out folds were introduced on September 3, so treat them as optimistic.
